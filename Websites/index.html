Customer Churn & Retention Drivers Analysis

Power BI Dashboard PostgreSQL Python 3.11

An end-to-end business intelligence project evaluating customer churn patterns, identifying high-risk customer segments, and delivering actionable retention strategies for recurring service models.

📌 Executive Summary

  • Business Problem: Customer churn directly erodes recurring revenue. Management lacked visibility into early risk indicators and contract-level attrition drivers.
  • Key Insight: Churn risk concentrates heavily in month-to-month contracts within the first 12 months of tenure, specifically among customers without automated payment options.
  • Actionable Recommendation: Implement automated churn-risk flags at Month 9 for month-to-month subscribers and introduce targeted tenure-based incentives to transition customers to annual agreements.

📊 Key Findings & Business Impact

Metric / Dimension Observation Strategic Action
Contract Type Month-to-month subscribers represent ~42% of total churn. Offer a 10% discount on 12-month contract conversions.
Payment Method Electronic check users exhibit 2.5x higher churn vs. automatic bank transfer. Incentivize auto-pay setup with account credits.
Service Inquiries Customers with 4+ customer service tickets churn at >65% rate. Route high-ticket accounts to dedicated customer success agents.

⚙️ Data Pipeline & Methodology

[ Raw Customer Data ] ──► [ PostgreSQL / SQL Cleaning ] ──► [ Python Exploratory Analysis ] ──► [ Power BI Star Schema ] ──► [ Executive Dashboard ]
  1. Data Extraction & Ingestion: Ingested customer transaction records, service tickets, and billing histories into PostgreSQL.
  2. Data Cleaning & Preprocessing (SQL & Python): Treated missing values, engineered tenure cohorts (0-12m, 12-24m, 24m+), and aggregated support ticket counts.
  3. Exploratory Data Analysis: Conducted correlation analysis in Python (pandas, seaborn) to pinpoint primary churn indicators.
  4. BI Modeling & DAX Measures: Built dynamic DAX measures for Churn Rate %, Lifetime Value (LTV), and Revenue at Risk.

Operational Yield & Product Efficiency Optimization

Microsoft Excel Power Query ETL VBA Automation

A practical operations analytics project leveraging advanced Excel, Power Query, and dynamic modeling to isolate process bottlenecks, monitor equipment yield variances, and drive operational efficiency gains.

📌 Executive Summary

  • Business Problem: Operational facility logs revealed inconsistent product yield output, resulting in inflated material waste and unaccounted machine downtime across facility shifts.
  • Key Insight: Yield variance was disproportionately tied to Shift B operational cycles, where preventive maintenance schedules lagged behind heavy usage runs.

Workforce Dynamics & Layoff Pattern Analysis

PostgreSQL / MySQL EDA Queries Data Cleaning

An end-to-end SQL data cleaning and exploratory data analysis (EDA) project evaluating global tech workforce downsizing, sector-specific impacts, and capital raised vs. headcount reduction dynamics.

📌 Executive Summary

  • Business Problem: Global economic shifts caused widespread, unstructured workforce reductions. Raw layoff datasets contained heavy noise, duplicates, inconsistent dates, and missing values.
  • Key Insight: Layoffs peaked in post-funding tech sectors, with total capital raised directly correlating with larger, single-event workforce reductions during interest rate spikes.
  • Actionable Recommendation: Develop sector-specific headcount buffer models for high-growth firms to stabilize workforce management during macroeconomic contraction cycles.

🔍 Analytical Highlights (SQL Sample)

WITH Industry_Year AS (
    SELECT 
        industry,
        EXTRACT(YEAR FROM date) AS layoff_year,
        SUM(total_laid_off) AS total_off
    FROM layoffs_staging2
    WHERE industry IS NOT NULL
    GROUP BY industry, EXTRACT(YEAR FROM date)
),
Industry_Rank AS (
    SELECT 
        industry,
        layoff_year,
        total_off,
        DENSE_RANK() OVER (PARTITION BY layoff_year ORDER BY total_off DESC) AS ranking
    FROM Industry_Year
)
SELECT * 
FROM Industry_Rank 
WHERE ranking <= 3;

⚙️ SQL Data Pipeline & Methodology

  1. Data Cleaning & Staging: Utilized ROW_NUMBER() window functions over (company, location, industry, total_laid_off, date) to isolate and remove duplicate rows. Standardized industry titles and string dates.
  2. Exploratory Data Analysis: Calculated rolling month-over-month cumulative workforce reductions using windowed SUM() OVER(ORDER BY month) and evaluated company funding stages.
